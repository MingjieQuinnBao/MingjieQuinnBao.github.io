Audio Perception Study

You will complete one practice task and two formal audio tasks. The study takes about 4 minutes.

Use your first listening impression. Do not carefully analyze the audio.

No special knowledge is needed.

Before you begin:
  • Please use headphones or earphones.
  • Use a quiet environment.
  • Set the volume to a comfortable, moderately low level.
  • Keep approximately the same moderately low volume throughout the study.
  • Please do not use audio-analysis software, spectrum analyzers, AI assistants, or other external tools.
  • Your compensation does not depend on whether your answers are correct.

Participation is voluntary. Stop immediately if the audio feels uncomfortable. The study does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or other directly identifying information.

Task Instructions

Each audio contains several distinct, clearly audible sequences.

Use your first listening impression. Do not carefully analyze the audio.

Count the number of distinct, clearly audible sequences. Count each continuous sequence once. A very brief interruption within one sequence does not create a new sequence. Enter one whole number, such as 5.

Do not count the spoken instruction. Begin counting after the pause that follows the instruction.
