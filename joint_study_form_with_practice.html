Combined Audio Perception Study

You will complete two practice examples and two combined audio challenges. The study takes about 7 minutes.

Use your first listening impression. Do not carefully analyze the audio.

Before you begin:
  • Use headphones or earphones in a quiet environment.
  • Set the volume to a comfortable, moderately low level.
  • Some audio may feel uncomfortable. If you experience discomfort, stop and exit the study.
  • Do not use audio-analysis software, AI assistants, or other external tools.
  • Your compensation does not depend on whether your answers are correct.

Task Instructions

Each combined challenge has two different audio tasks.

Pattern Task

You will hear four sounds in order. Classify the overall character of each sound and enter four letters in the same order.

R = Rising, F = Falling,
L = Stable / no clear trend, N = Noise.
Section Count Task

After the spoken instruction, count how many clearly audible sound sections you hear. Enter one whole number.
You may enter or update your answer while the audio is playing. You can continue only after the complete audio has finished.

Use your first listening impression. Do not carefully analyze the audio.
